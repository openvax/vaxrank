Candidate score calculations

The expression is taken from each candidate. Its target_epitope_score sums the stored scores of retained target occurrences; With MS scoring, this is the baseline allele sum plus one maximum MS increment per unique peptide sequence in the window. The MS transform is B^(1/G), where G = 1 + gain × log2(1 + max(R, P, J)); these are heuristic ranking scores, not probabilities. Without MS scoring, each occurrence sums its credited allele scores. This is distinct from a window utility that uses unique sequence/allele pairs. Presentation, WT comparison, cleavage, RNA support and manufacturability affect this expression only when its recorded terms use them. Admission and window-selection constraints may act separately.
