Candidate score calculations

The expression is taken from each candidate. Its target_epitope_score sums the stored scores of retained target occurrences; each occurrence score sums its credited allele scores. This is distinct from a window utility that uses unique sequence/allele pairs. Presentation, WT comparison, cleavage, RNA support and manufacturability affect this expression only when its recorded terms use them. Admission and window-selection constraints may act separately.
